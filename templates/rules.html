Game Rules

Brickfall is a classic Breakout arcade game.

How to Play

  1. Move the paddle using left and right arrows.
  2. Press Space to launch the ball.
  3. Destroy bricks to earn points.
  4. Complete all three levels to win.
  5. Don't lose all three lives.

Controls

Left / Right - Move paddle

Space - Launch ball

P - Pause
