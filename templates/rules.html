A SMALL GUIDE

Game Rules

Keep the ball in play. Clear every brick.

The goal

Move the paddle to bounce the ball into the bricks. Complete all three levels to win. You start with three lives and lose one when the ball falls below the paddle. The ball moves faster on each level.

Each brick is worth 10 points. A completed run is sent to the server under your player name. Your best score earns your place on the leaderboard.

Controls

← → or A D
Move the paddle
Space
Launch the ball
Shift
Slow the ball, once per level
P
Pause or continue
Esc
Pause; press again to return to the menu
F5
Pause and save your current game
F9
Load your saved game from the menu or pause screen

Two special mechanics

01

A narrower paddle

After every five destroyed bricks, the paddle becomes narrower for three seconds. It then returns to its normal width.

02

A moment to slow down

Press Shift while the ball is moving to slow it for three seconds. You get one use per level. Losing a life does not restore that use.

Your progress

Register or sign in inside the Pygame application. Save an unfinished game to your account, then load it later. The website shows scores and player statistics from the same server database.
