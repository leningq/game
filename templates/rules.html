Rules

Keep the ball in play. Clear every brick.

The goal

Bounce the ball with your paddle and clear all three levels. You start with three lives; each missed ball costs one. Every brick earns 10 points.

Sign in inside the game to save your progress and send your completed games to the leaderboard.

Controls

← → or A D
Move the paddle
Space
Launch the ball
Shift
Slow the ball, once per level
P or Esc
Pause; press P to continue or Esc to return to the menu
F5
Pause and save your current game
F9
Load your saved game from the menu or pause screen

Two small twists

A narrower paddle

Every five destroyed bricks make the paddle narrower for three seconds. It then returns to its normal width.

A moment to slow down

Press Shift while the ball is moving to slow it for three seconds. You get one use per level. Losing a life does not restore it.
